### Company Overview
We are SES (Spartan Energy Services), a licensed contractor working with utility companies on their Net Metering program. We are one of several companies operating under this program — this is the utility company's program, not exclusively ours. We help homeowners go solar by scheduling in-home consultations (appointments) with a closer who will assess the home and present financing options.

### Booking Requirements
- Roof size: 700 sq ft minimum
- Sun hours: 1,300 minimum
- Exception: If roof is 600 sq ft or higher AND sun hours are very strong, the customer can provide a screenshot in the support chat and we may grant permission to book.
- Exception: If roof is 700+ sq ft AND the electric bill is above $250, the appointment can still be booked — provided the homeowner is willing to have their trees trimmed or removed.

Appointments that do not meet these criteria should not be booked.

### Disqualification Criteria
- Not a homeowner
- For MD/IL/PA: if the address contains a PO Box or apartment, mark as DNQ immediately — do not engage with the lead
- We don't service their utility company
- High or medium shade and not willing to cut trees
- High or medium shading and electric bill is below $250
- About to get their roof replaced
- Already has solar AND electric bill is less than $100 — DQ them
- Already has solar but receives an electric bill from their utility company greater than $100 — we CAN help them
- Credit score below 670 (recently verified), and no other household member with a 670 or higher

### Credit Score Requirements

**Above 670:**
Meets the requirements. Proceed with booking.

**Below 670:**
Ask: "When was the last time you checked your score?"
- If their score was above 650 when last checked 2–3 months ago → Book them in. Inform the closer in the notes.
- If their score was above 650 when last checked 1–2 months ago → Book them in. Inform the closer in the notes.

In both cases, also ask:
"Would there happen to be anyone who lives with you that would have a 670 or higher?"
- If not sure: "No worries — would they be home at the time of the appointment?" → Move on, put "not sure" on the Jotform.
- If certain no one in the household is above 670: "Unfortunately, from everything you're sharing, this program is something that you would not be able to qualify for. If there is another program in the future that would make more sense for you, we will be sure to reach out. Thank you for your time, it was a pleasure speaking with you!"

**Not sure of credit score:**
Ask: "Would you say it's likely above or below 670?"
- If still not sure: "Would there happen to be anyone who lives at your home that may have a 670 or higher?"
  - If not sure about family member: "No worries" → Move on, put "not sure" on the Jotform.
  - If certain no one is above 670 → Use the disqualification script above.
